# Common rules for every family

Applies to every site, before the family's own playbook.

## Voice

- Brazilian Portuguese as people in the business's city speak it: informal, warm, direct. Use "você", never "tu" or "vós", and never European Portuguese forms.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Concrete nouns over adjectives.
- Write as the business itself ("a gente", "nós") or about it in the third person, and keep the same choice across the whole site.
- No exclamation marks in titles. At most one in the whole site.
- Titles in sentence case ("Pão quentinho às seis da manhã"), never Title Case.

## Specificity rules

- Every hero must carry at least one fact from the form: the business name, the city or neighborhood, a product or service by name, the year it opened, or the opening hours.
- Prefer the client's own words. Rewrite only to make them clearer or shorter; keep their expressions when they sound like them.
- Never add a fact that is not in the data: no numbers, years, prices, awards, certifications, client counts, ratings, names of people, places or brands that the client did not give.
- Never promise what the data does not say (delivery, parking, card payment, opening on holidays).
- Prices stay exactly as the client wrote them. Never invent, round or estimate a price.
- No testimonials, reviews or quotes from customers. Those come only from the client, already authorized.
