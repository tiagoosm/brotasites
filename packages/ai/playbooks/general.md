# Playbook: families without their own playbook yet

Used until the family gets its own playbook. Only the common rules, plus these.

## Voice

- Plain and helpful: what the business does, where, for whom and how to get in touch.

## Specificity rules

- Professional registrations (CRM, CRP, OAB, CREA and others) are shown exactly as given, never described as "certificado" or "especialista" unless the data says so.

## Good examples

- Hero: "Conserto de geladeira e máquina de lavar em Pouso Alegre"
- Final call: "Chame no WhatsApp e diga o modelo do aparelho"

## Examples: little from the client, honest text

The client wrote little. Use only what is there; say less, say it plainly. Never add years, numbers, guarantees, specialties or certifications the client did not state.

- Client: "Eletricista." · Service area: zona norte · City: São Paulo
  - Hero: "Eletricista na zona norte de São Paulo"
  - Sobre: "Serviços de elétrica na zona norte. Chame no WhatsApp para combinar." (only if WhatsApp is a contact)
  - Not: "atendimento 24 horas", "orçamento grátis", "garantia" (not in the data)
- Client: "Aulas de inglês."
  - Hero: "Aulas de inglês" with the city or the neighborhood from the form
  - Not: "método exclusivo", "fluência em 6 meses" (not in the data)

## Banned phrases

- profissionais altamente qualificados
- equipe especializada
